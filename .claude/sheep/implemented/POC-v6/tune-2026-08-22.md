# /sheep tune — easy + flank (2026-08-22)

**Target:** the POC-v6 dog rules — **I2** (easy = a slow move forward, ~⅓
walk-on), **C8** (flank tracks the perimeter to the gate line; standoff,
reach bound) and the two touch-up smoothings (`fenceSeekRamp`,
`parkFade`). Owner-directed ("do all the rest of the things on your list"),
run **headless** per the no-webserver rule — so these are measured facts, not
feel; the feel read is the owner's.

**Method:** whole script under the DOM/lil-gui stub, regression anchor
(forced `beside-gate`, `spawnGateClear 0`, `randomSeedOnLoad` off, levers
0.5), seeds **1337 / 42**, commands after 0.5 s idle; one family at a time,
bracketed. Baseline first.

## Adopted changes

**None.** Every family either sits at its measured knee already or trades
one thing for another with no defect at the default. The one real finding is
a mechanism, not a value — parked as **Q38** below, not bent mid-session.

## Findings by family

### Family 1 — `easySpeedFrac` (I2) — **finding, parked as Q38**

20 s from the handoff tick, dog left in easy:

| frac | reached the trail gap (1337 / 42) | GCM toward goal in 20 s | panic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 | 4.0 s / 2.8 s | −7 / −10 px | 0.14 / 0.53 % |
| 0.33 (default) | 4.0 s / 2.8 s | −6 / +5 px | 0.14 / 0.53 % |
| 0.5 | 4.0 s / 2.8 s | −1 / 0 px | 0.14 / 0.53 % |
| 0.67 | 4.0 s / 2.8 s | +5 / −1 px | 0.24 / 0.53 % |
| 1.0 | 4.0 s / 2.8 s | +8 / 0 px | 0.22 / 0.53 % |
| walk-on issued at handoff | 1.6 s / 1.1 s | −22 / −43 px | 0.03 / 0.38 % |

The time to reach the gap is **identical at every speed**: after a handoff
the dog already stands on the ring, and his approach is paced by the E1
governor's `driveDist` shrink — a hard-coded **25 px/s** inside `stepDog`
(`d.driveDist - 25 * dt`), which a 22 px/s dog (0.2 × 110) can still keep
up with. So from the gate line **easy and walk-on close at the same pace**;
I2's "a third of walk-on" is only visible when the dog is *far* from his
trailing spot (P1 measured it there: 36.3 vs 110 px/s). The GCM-toward-goal
column is ≈ 0 for all because the beside-gate flock is already against the
fence beside the mouth — that is POC-v2 Q8 / G1 territory, not this family.

### Family 3 — `flankHandoffDeg` (C8 reach bound) — inert on a fresh flank

10 / 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 → handoff times, carries and sweeps **identical to the
decimal**. The flank point parks on the tick its progress crosses 0, which is
inside any bound ≥ ~1°; the bound only matters for a command issued when the
dog is *already* past the line (then it decides park-now vs go-round).
Documented in Appendix A.3; no change — it is a legitimate "how far past the
line still counts" dial, just not one that shapes an ordinary flank.
